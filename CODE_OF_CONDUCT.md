# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone participating in this project — issues, PRs, reviews, chat — is
expected to keep the space technical, respectful, and free of harassment.

## Standards

**Encouraged:**
- Focusing criticism on code and design decisions, not people
- Concrete reproductions, benchmarks, and counter-examples
- Accepting that your patch may be reworked, and engaging with why

**Unacceptable:**
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Publishing others' private information
- Trolling, thread-hijacking, or bad-faith arguments

## Enforcement

Violations in project spaces should be reported via a GitHub report to the
maintainers. Confirmed violations lead to comment removal, temporary bans,
or permanent exclusion at maintainers' discretion.
